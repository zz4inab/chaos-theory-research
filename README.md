# Chaos Theory: From Simple Maps to Chua's Circuit

## Overview

This project investigates how simple deterministic systems
can generate complex and chaotic behaviour through
nonlinearity and sensitivity to initial conditions.

The research progresses from simple one-dimensional maps to
continuous-time systems and finally to an engineering
application in Chua's circuit.

## Research Progression

| Week | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Chaos Foundations — Circle & Doubling Maps |
| 2 | Logistic Map & Route to Chaos |
| 3 | Lorenz System & Butterfly Effect |
| 4 | Chua's Circuit & Engineering Application |

## Key Concepts

- Deterministic dynamical systems
- Nonlinearity
- Bifurcations
- Period-doubling
- Feigenbaum constant
- Lyapunov exponents
- Sensitive dependence on initial conditions
- Strange attractors
- Continuous-time chaos

## Computational Methods

The simulations were developed in Python using:

- NumPy
- SciPy
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Engineering Application

The final stage of the project investigates Chua's circuit
as a physical example of deterministic chaos and examines
its characteristic double-scroll attractor.
